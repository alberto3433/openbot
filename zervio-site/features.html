Natural Language Processing

Understands Orders the Way People Actually Speak

No rigid forms or button-clicking. Customers describe what they want in plain language, and Zervio captures every item, modifier, and preference with precision.

  • ✓ Multi-item orders in a single sentence
  • ✓ Complex modifiers — "extra cream cheese, no onion"
  • ✓ Contextual follow-ups — "make that iced" or "add another"
  • ✓ Alias recognition — "BEC", "the usual", "large coffee"
