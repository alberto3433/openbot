FAQ

Common Questions

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a week. We handle the menu configuration and integration setup — your team just reviews and approves.

Do I need technical expertise to manage Zervio?

Not at all. Menu updates, pricing changes, and availability controls are all managed through a simple dashboard. No code required.

What POS systems do you integrate with?

We currently support Toast and Square, with more integrations on our roadmap. We also offer a webhook API for custom integrations.

Is there a minimum contract or commitment?

We offer flexible terms including month-to-month options. Reach out and we'll find the right plan for your business.
